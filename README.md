# DPI Consultants | Premium Student Division

Official website for **DPI Consultants (Des Pardes International)** Premium Student Division, offering personalised global university admissions guidance, scholarship research, student profile development, and portfolio support for ambitious students in Pakistan.

## Pages Included
- `index.html`: Home (Overview, Core Capabilities, Student & Parent Guidance, Four-Stage Journey Timeline)
- `admissions.html`: University Admissions (United States & United Kingdom Admissions, Individualised Strategy)
- `scholarships.html`: Scholarships & Funding (Funding Pathways, 5-Step Support Process, Advisory Transparency)
- `profile-building.html`: Profile & Portfolio Building (6 Structured Services, Academic & Creative Tracks)
- `about.html`: About DPI Consultants (Des Pardes International, Premium Student Division, Our Approach & Commitment)
- `consultation.html`: Book a Consultation (Session Agenda & Interactive Student/Parent Consultation Form)
- `styles.css`: Editorial Academic Prestige Design System
- `main.js`: Interactive Navigation & Consultation Brief Controller
