About DPI Consultants · Des Pardes International

Guidance That Begins With Your Goals.

DPI Consultants, short for Des Pardes International, is an education consultancy that provides information and counselling on international university admissions and student visa applications. We help students understand their study-abroad options, navigate application requirements, and make informed decisions about pursuing education overseas.

Through our counselling and admissions guidance, we aim to make the process clearer for students and their families, from exploring universities and understanding entry requirements to preparing applications and navigating the relevant visa process.

Specialised Advisory

Introducing Our Premium Student Division

Our premium student division builds on this foundation with a more specialised focus on ambitious students seeking competitive university admissions, scholarship opportunities, and structured profile development.

We bring together university research, application planning, scholarship guidance, portfolio development, and personalised student support within a coordinated approach.

Whether your goal is to explore selective universities in the United States, consider leading UK institutions, identify funding opportunities, or develop a stronger academic and extracurricular profile, our focus is on helping you understand your options and prepare strategically.

Guiding Principles

Our Approach

Personalised Guidance

Every student has a different academic background, set of interests, financial circumstances, and future ambitions. We begin by understanding yours.

Strategic Planning

We help students identify priorities, understand requirements, and plan applications around relevant deadlines and realistic options.

Transparency

We explain processes, eligibility requirements, costs, and limitations so that students and families can make informed decisions.

Long-Term Thinking

Where relevant, we encourage students to begin early, develop meaningful experiences, and approach university applications as a process rather than a last-minute task.

Our Commitment

Our Commitment

We believe that every student deserves clear guidance and an honest understanding of their opportunities. Our role is to support informed decisions, careful preparation, and a more structured approach to international education.
