United States Admissions

Think Beyond the Name. Build Your Application Around You.

Common App · Ivy League & Selective US

US admissions can involve more than grades and test scores. Depending on the institution, applicants may need to demonstrate academic preparation, extracurricular engagement, leadership, personal interests, and a clear sense of purpose.

Our US admissions support can include:

  • ✓ University and programme research.
  • ✓ Ivy League and other selective university exploration.
  • ✓ Common App guidance and application planning.
  • ✓ Personal statement and supplemental essay guidance.
  • ✓ Activities list and achievements presentation.
  • ✓ SAT/ACT requirement research and application planning.
  • ✓ Scholarship and financial aid research.
  • ✓ Application deadline and document tracking.
The goal: to help you prepare an authentic, well-organised application that reflects your individual strengths and fits the requirements of your selected institutions.
United Kingdom Admissions

A More Focused Route to Your Next Degree.

UCAS · Russell Group & UK Universities

The UK offers a wide range of internationally recognised universities and specialised academic programmes. Choosing the right course and presenting your academic motivation clearly are essential parts of the application process.

Our UK admissions support can include:

  • ✓ Russell Group and other university research.
  • ✓ Course selection and entry requirement assessment.
  • ✓ UCAS application guidance.
  • ✓ Personal statement guidance aligned with the relevant application requirements.
  • ✓ Academic document and reference coordination.
  • ✓ Application deadline planning.
  • ✓ Scholarship research and offer review.
The goal: to help you make informed course choices and submit an application that clearly demonstrates your academic suitability and motivation.
